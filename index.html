About

I'm a Principal Microsoft Cloud Architect who believes the best architecture is invisible—working quietly in the background while teams move fast and users stay productive. Over the past decade, I've focused on designing Microsoft 365 and Azure environments that scale reliably without constant intervention.

My work centers on the foundational pieces that make everything else possible: identity architecture with Entra ID, Zero Trust security frameworks, endpoint platforms that just work, and governance standards that reduce complexity instead of adding to it. I've built multi-tenant environments, modernized legacy platforms, and established architectural patterns that help operational teams succeed.

Right now, I'm looking for my next role—specifically positions where architecture and strategy are distinct from day-to-day operations. I want to focus on platform vision, security design, and technical standards rather than firefighting and escalations. If your organization values strategic architecture work, I'd welcome a conversation.
