About

I believe the best architecture is invisible. It works quietly in the background while teams move fast and users stay productive. For the past decade, I've been designing Microsoft 365 and Azure environments that scale reliably without constant intervention.

Most of my work focuses on the foundational pieces that make everything else possible. Identity architecture with Entra ID. Zero Trust security frameworks. Endpoint platforms that just work. Governance standards that reduce complexity instead of adding to it. I've built multi-tenant environments, modernized legacy platforms, and created architectural patterns that help operational teams succeed.

I'm looking for my next role, specifically positions where architecture and strategy are distinct from day-to-day operations. I want to focus on platform vision, security design, and technical standards rather than firefighting and escalations. If your organization values strategic architecture work, I'd welcome a conversation.
